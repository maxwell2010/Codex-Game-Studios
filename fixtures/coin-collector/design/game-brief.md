# Coin Collector

Small offline, single-player 2D keyboard game. Move a teal player around an arena,
collect three gold coins, reach a victory state and restart. Godot 4.5.1,
GDScript, desktop, compatibility renderer. No network, accounts, saves, telemetry,
downloads or third-party assets. All gameplay tuning is in tuning.json.

Use minimal rigor, autonomous execution within the authorized test project,
and no platform certification (internal source-project release). No GDD or ADR
is required at this tier. The goal is to validate the studio lifecycle and game
behavior, not assess commercial viability or player enjoyment.

Out of scope: combat, progression, audio, mobile, storefronts and packaged exports.
